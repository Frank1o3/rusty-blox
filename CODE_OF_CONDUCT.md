# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make this project’s community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to respecting the dignity, rights, and contributions of everyone, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. Everyone who participates in good faith and follows this Code of Conduct is entitled to respectful treatment.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

1. Respect the purpose of the project and its community spaces.
2. Engage kindly and honestly with other people.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for their actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Acknowledge and repair harm when it occurs.
7. Help maintain a community where technical disagreement does not become personal hostility.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained unwanted personal attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, demeaning remarks, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing or misusing another person’s private or confidential information without permission.
- Impersonation, including impersonation intended to evade moderation.
- Encouraging violence, self-harm, or other harm toward a person or group.
- Deliberately disrupting project collaboration, issue tracking, or review.
- Submitting work without appropriate attribution or misrepresenting another person’s work as your own.
- Posting promotional or commercial material unrelated to the project in project spaces.

Disagreement about design, implementation, licensing, compatibility, or project direction is permitted. Keep criticism focused on the work, evidence, and technical reasoning.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-maintained spaces. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public or at an event.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation to the repository maintainer, [@Frank1o3](https://github.com/Frank1o3). Use GitHub’s private reporting options where available. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue or pull request. Include links or relevant context where safe to do so.

Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer. Reports and enforcement details will be handled as privately as is practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and pattern of behavior, the maintainer may issue a warning, request corrective action, limit participation temporarily, or remove someone from project spaces. Serious threats, harassment, doxxing, or repeated violations may result in immediate removal. Enforcement decisions will be based on the available context and will aim to protect participants and the project.
